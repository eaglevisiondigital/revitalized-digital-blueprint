A mission born from
real experience.

His passion for health began at six years old, watching his father transform lives through fitness. His own journey through hardship, addiction, anxiety, chronic fatigue and restoration shaped a conviction that transformation must reach far beyond appearance.

Her path through trauma-related health challenges, malnourishment, body dysmorphia and the modeling industry built a passion for helping people discover lasting health, confidence, healing and purpose.

ReVitalized Academy exists because two people experienced genuine transformation and felt called to help others experience the same freedom.
